Frequently Asked Questions

When will merit list be published?
The merit list is scheduled to be published on 15th August 2025. It will be available on this portal and the college notice board.
How do I pay the admission fee?
After the merit list is published, you can pay the admission fee through the Fee Payment tab on your dashboard using UPI, Net Banking, or DD.
What documents are required?
Required documents include: Passport photo, Signature, SSC marksheet, HSC marksheet, Leaving certificate, Aadhaar card, Category certificate (if applicable), Income certificate, Domicile certificate.
How to check application status?
Log in to your dashboard and check the Application Status tab. You can also enable notifications to receive updates via email/SMS.
