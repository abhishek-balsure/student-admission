About Sarhad College

Welcome to Sarhad College

Sarhad College of Arts, Commerce & Science is a premier educational institution located in Katraj, Pune. Established with a vision to provide quality education to students from all backgrounds, we strive to empower the next generation with knowledge, skills, and values.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and quality education that transforms students into responsible citizens and competent professionals.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in education, research, and community service, nurturing students to achieve their full potential.

Key Highlights

  • Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University
  • NAAC Accredited 'B+' Grade
  • UGC 2(f) & 12(B) Recognized
  • Well-qualified faculty
  • Modern infrastructure and facilities
  • Regular academic and extra-curricular activities
